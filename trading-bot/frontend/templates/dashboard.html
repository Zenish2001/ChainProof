How it works

Every few minutes the bot pulls the BTC price and the last 300 daily candles from Coinbase. Four indicators (RSI, MACD, a 20/50-day moving average crossover and Bollinger Bands) each vote buy, sell or hold.

The numbers on this page come from this live run only. They are separate from the backtest results, which are published, hashed and stored on-chain in ChainProof.

Made by Zenish Borad. Python, pandas, Flask, Coinbase API.
